# How to get teens to actually post in your Hack Club program's channel. 

A lot of high quality Hack Club YSWS (You Ship We Ship) programs are facing a real issue in their channel: nobody talks. This shouldn't be taken lightly as message activity creates strong engagement around your program. As I've observed, a 10k users flagship program with 60% fewer messages posted in its channel than a 2k users community-orged program will have a fairly weaker community, which is regrettable for a flagship. As we all know, Hack Club is a place for teenagers to create cool projects and make friends, that's why a strong community will make your program memorable and those teens in 10 years will actualy talk about Hack Club as a beautiful place, thanks to YOUR program.

## WHat is a Show & Tell

Shows & Tells are events that regularly happen in [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com)'s community. In them, teens get to showcase and talk about one of their projects for a few minutes, while other teens listen to the presentation and get inspired. 

From what I've seen, they are one of the best ways to improve the quality of conversations in the program's channel which they are organized for. Teens talking proudly about the projects they just finished to other teens is the perfect way to bring up good energy, positive reactions and even motivation for those other teens to keep it up with projects. This will start discussions about the projects seen, how did they do it, what for, how to recreate it, and these discussions would last even after the Show & Tell ends.
